General California rules for adults and medicinal customers, each with its official source.

Getting a Medical CardFAQ

California

The rules in brief

  1. 01

    Adults 21 and older can buy cannabis from state-licensed retailers with a valid government-issued photo ID.

    Source: cannabis.ca.gov (opens in a new tab)

  2. 02

    Adults 21 and older may possess up to 28.5 grams of cannabis and up to 8 grams of concentrated cannabis.

    Source: California Health and Safety Code 11362.1

  3. 03

    Medicinal cannabis customers must be 18 or older and hold a valid physician's recommendation.

    Source: California Business and Professions Code 26140

  4. 04

    Smoking or vaping cannabis anywhere tobacco smoking is banned, and consuming cannabis in public places, is not allowed.

    Source: California Health and Safety Code 11362.3

  5. 05

    Never drive impaired; cannabis in a vehicle must be in a sealed container or the trunk.

    Source: California Vehicle Code 23152, 23222

  6. 06

    Check that a business is licensed before you buy: the state lists every licence holder.

    Source: search.cannabis.ca.gov (opens in a new tab)
